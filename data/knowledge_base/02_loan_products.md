# SecureBank Loan Products

## Home Loan

SecureBank Home Loans help you purchase, construct, or renovate your dream home.

**Key Details:**
- Loan amount: $500,000 – $50,000,000
- Interest rate: 8.50% p.a. (floating, linked to repo rate)
- Maximum tenure: 30 years (360 months)
- Minimum CIBIL score: 700
- Processing fee: 0.5% of loan amount (max $25,000)

**Eligibility:**
- Age: 21–65 years (at loan maturity)
- Salaried or self-employed with minimum 2 years of stable income
- Property must be located in India

**Features:**
- Zero prepayment penalty on floating-rate loans
- Balance transfer facility from other banks
- Top-up loan up to 70% of property value
- Digital documentation — no physical visits required
- PMAY subsidy eligibility for first-time buyers

## Personal Loan

Unsecured loans for any personal need — medical, travel, education, or home renovation.

**Key Details:**
- Loan amount: $10,000 – $2,000,000
- Interest rate: 11.99%–18% p.a. (based on creditworthiness)
- Maximum tenure: 5 years (60 months)
- Minimum CIBIL score: 650
- Processing fee: 2% of loan amount

**Eligibility:**
- Salaried individuals: minimum salary $25,000/month
- Self-employed: minimum annual profit $300,000
- Age: 23–55 years

**Features:**
- Instant disbursal within 24 hours for pre-approved customers
- No collateral or guarantor required
- Flexible repayment options (EMI via NACH mandate)
- Part-prepayment allowed after 6 EMIs with 2% charge

## Auto Loan

Finance your new or pre-owned vehicle with competitive rates.

**Key Details:**
- Loan amount: $100,000 – $5,000,000
- Interest rate: 9.25% p.a.
- Maximum tenure: 7 years (84 months)
- Minimum CIBIL score: 680
- Processing fee: 1% of loan amount

**Eligibility:**
- For new vehicles: up to 100% on-road funding
- For used vehicles (up to 5 years old): up to 80% funding
- Age: 21–60 years

**Features:**
- Quick approval in 2 hours for existing SecureBank customers
- Doorstep document collection
- Zero foreclosure charges after 12 months

## Loan Against Fixed Deposit (LAFD)

Borrow against your existing FD without breaking it.

**Key Details:**
- Up to 90% of FD value
- Interest rate: FD rate + 2%
- No CIBIL score requirement
- Instant disbursal

## How to Apply
1. Log in to SecureBank app or website
2. Choose "Apply for Loan" from the dashboard
3. Fill in the application form and upload documents
4. Get an instant in-principle approval
5. Complete verification and receive disbursal

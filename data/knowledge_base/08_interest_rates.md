# SecureBank Interest Rate Policy

## Savings Account Interest Rates
| Balance Tier | Interest Rate (p.a.) |
|---|---|
| Up to $100,000 | 3.00% |
| $100,001 – $500,000 | 3.50% |
| Above $500,000 | 4.00% |

Interest is calculated daily on end-of-day balance and credited quarterly.

- Rate: 7.75% p.a.

## Overdraft / Credit Line Rates
- Savings overdraft: 12% p.a.
- Current account overdraft: 13% p.a.
- Credit card revolving credit: 3.4% per month (40.8% p.a.)

## Repo Rate & Monetary Policy
SecureBank's floating-rate loans are linked to RBI's repo rate. When RBI changes the repo rate, floating loan EMIs are adjusted within 3 months.

**Current repo rate (as of the last policy review):** 6.50%

## TDS on Interest Income
- TDS @ 10% deducted if interest income > $40,000 per year from FDs
- Submit Form 15G/15H to avoid TDS if income is below taxable limit

# SecureBank Fraud Prevention & Security

## Our Security Commitment
SecureBank employs multi-layered security measures to protect your accounts and transactions 24/7.

## Common Fraud Scenarios

### 1. Phishing
Fraudsters send fake emails or SMS messages impersonating SecureBank to steal credentials.

**How to stay safe:**
- SecureBank will NEVER ask for your password, OTP, or full card number via email, SMS, or phone.
- Always check the sender email domain: official emails come only from @securebank.com
- Do not click links in unsolicited emails. Visit securebank.com directly.

### 2. SIM Swap Fraud
Criminals port your mobile number to a new SIM to intercept OTPs.

**Warning signs:**
- Sudden loss of mobile network signal
- Unexpected OTPs arriving for unknown transactions

**Action:** Call SecureBank immediately at 1800-SECURE-1 if you suspect SIM swap.

### 3. Card Skimming
ATM card readers may be tampered with to copy card data.

**Prevention:**
- Always cover the keypad when entering your PIN
- Inspect ATM card readers for loose or unusual attachments
- Use ATMs located inside bank branches or well-lit areas

### 4. UPI / Online Payment Fraud
Fraudsters may send collect requests or create fake payment links.

**Remember:**
- You do NOT need to enter a PIN or OTP to RECEIVE money
- Never scan a QR code to receive funds
- Verify the recipient's UPI ID before making payments

## What to Do If You Suspect Fraud

1. **Immediately freeze your card** via the SecureBank mobile app
2. **Change your password and MPIN** from a secure device
3. **Call our 24/7 fraud hotline:** 1800-SECURE-1 (toll-free)
4. **File a police complaint** and share the FIR copy with SecureBank
5. **Raise a dispute** within 48 hours for best chance of recovery

## Zero Liability Policy
Customers are not liable for unauthorized transactions if:
- The fraud is reported within 3 days
- The customer did not share credentials or OTPs
- The account is in good standing

Partial liability (up to $10,000) may apply for delays up to 7 days.

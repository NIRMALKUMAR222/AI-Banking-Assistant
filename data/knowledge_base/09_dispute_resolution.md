# SecureBank Dispute Resolution & Grievance Redressal

## Types of Disputes

### 1. Transaction Disputes
- Incorrect/duplicate charges
- Failed ATM cash dispensation
- Unauthorized card transactions
- UPI payment not received by beneficiary

### 2. Service Complaints
- Poor customer service quality
- Branch or staff misconduct
- Digital banking technical issues
- Account closure/opening delays

### 3. Loan-Related Disputes
- Incorrect EMI deduction
- Premature closure discrepancies
- Interest calculation errors

## How to Raise a Dispute

### Step 1: Contact Customer Care
- **App/Website:** Go to Help > Raise Dispute
- **Phone:** 1800-SECURE-1 (24x7, toll-free)
- **Email:** support@securebank.com
- **Branch:** Visit nearest branch for in-person resolution

**Expected resolution time:** 7 working days for most disputes.

### Step 2: Escalate to Grievance Officer
If unresolved within 7 days or unsatisfied with resolution:
- Email: grievance.officer@securebank.com
- Write to: Grievance Officer, SecureBank Ltd., Head Office, Mumbai – 400001
- **Resolution time:** 15 working days

### Step 3: Banking Ombudsman (RBI)
If unresolved by SecureBank within 30 days, escalate to RBI Banking Ombudsman:
- Online: cms.rbi.org.in
- Applicable for complaints involving amounts up to $20,000,000
- No fees charged for filing

## Failed ATM Transaction
If cash not dispensed but account debited:
- Auto-reversal within 5 working days (RBI mandate)
- If not reversed, raise dispute via app
- Compensation: $100/day delay beyond 5 days (credited automatically)

## Chargeback Process (Credit/Debit Card)
1. Raise dispute within 120 days of transaction
2. Provide transaction details and merchant correspondence
3. SecureBank files chargeback with card network
4. Provisional credit within 10 days while under review
5. Final resolution within 45–90 days

## RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme
Since November 2021, RBI's Integrated Ombudsman covers:
- Commercial banks
- NBFCs
- Payment system operators

All at one portal: cms.rbi.org.in

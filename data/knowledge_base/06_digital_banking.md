# SecureBank Digital Banking Features

## Mobile Banking App

Available on iOS and Android, the SecureBank app offers a full-featured banking experience.

### Core Features
- **Account Overview:** Real-time balances, mini-statement
- **Fund Transfers:** NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, UPI — all within the app
- **Bill Payments:** Electricity, water, gas, broadband, mobile recharge
- **Investments:** Fixed Deposits, Mutual Funds, Gold — instant creation
- **Loan Management:** EMI schedule, part-payment, foreclosure

### Smart Alerts
- Transaction notifications (instant, via push + SMS)
- Low balance alerts (configurable threshold)
- EMI due reminders
- Unusual activity alerts

### Security Controls
- Biometric login (fingerprint + Face ID)
- Card controls: freeze/unfreeze, set limits, enable/disable contactless
- Travel mode: enable international usage before traveling
- Virtual card generation for online transactions

## Internet Banking (securebank.com)
- Full transaction history (up to 7 years)
- Bulk payment uploads (for businesses)
- Detailed account statements (downloadable as PDF/Excel)
- Tax documents: Form 26AS, TDS certificates, interest certificates

## UPI Integration
SecureBank supports UPI via:
- SecureBank Pay app (native)
- All third-party UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm)
- UPI ID format: `yourname@securebank`

## NEFT / RTGS / IMPS Details
| Mode | Timing | Limit | Settlement |
|---|---|---|---|
| IMPS | 24x7x365 | $200,000 per txn | Instant |
| NEFT | 24x7x365 | No upper limit | Batch (30 min) |
| RTGS | 7 AM – 6 PM (Mon–Sat) | Min $200,000 | Instant |
| UPI | 24x7x365 | $100,000 per txn | Instant |

## Digital Onboarding
New customers can open accounts 100% digitally:
1. Download the app
2. Complete Video KYC (10 minutes)
3. E-sign documents via Aadhaar OTP
4. Account activated within 2 hours

## Chatbot & AI Assistant
- 24/7 AI assistant available in the app
- Handles FAQs, balance queries, mini-statements
- Escalates complex issues to a human agent within 2 minutes

# SecureBank International Transfers

## Overview
SecureBank provides fast, secure international wire transfers via SWIFT network to 150+ countries.

## Transfer Fees

| Transfer Amount | Fee |
|----------------|-----|
| Up to $10,000 | $500 flat |
| $10,001 – $50,000 | $1,000 flat |
| $50,001 – $500,000 | 0.15% of amount |
| Above $500,000 | Negotiable (contact your RM) |

**Foreign Currency Markup:** 1.5% over the interbank rate.
**SWIFT charges:** Correspondent bank fees may apply ($10–$30 additional).

## Transfer Limits
- Daily limit: $500,000 per account
- Single transaction limit: $250,000 (higher amounts require branch approval)
- Monthly limit: $2,000,000

## Processing Time
| Destination | Processing Time |
|---|---|
| USA, UK, EU, Singapore | 1–2 business days |
| Middle East, Australia | 2–3 business days |
| Africa, South America | 3–5 business days |
| Remote locations | 5–7 business days |

## Required Information for SWIFT Transfer
1. Beneficiary full name (as per bank records)
2. Beneficiary bank name and country
3. SWIFT/BIC code of beneficiary bank
4. IBAN or account number
5. Beneficiary address
6. Purpose of remittance (mandatory for RBI compliance)

## Permitted Purposes of Remittance (LRS)
Under RBI's Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS), individuals can remit up to $250,000 per year for:
- Education and living expenses abroad
- Medical treatment
- Travel
- Maintenance of close relatives
- Investments in foreign securities

**Not Permitted:** Lottery, margin trading, purchase of FCCBs from Indian companies.

## How to Send an International Transfer
1. Log in to SecureBank app or internet banking
2. Go to Payments > International Transfer
3. Add beneficiary with SWIFT details (one-time setup)
4. Enter amount and select currency
5. Choose purpose of remittance
6. Authenticate with OTP
7. Track status under "Transfer History"

## Currency Exchange
- Real-time exchange rates available in the app

- Multi-currency accounts available for frequent senders

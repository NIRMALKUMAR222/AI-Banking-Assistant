# SecureBank Terms & Conditions (Summary)

## Account Terms

### Account Ownership
- Accounts are non-transferable
- Joint accounts require all holders' signatures for closure
- Minor accounts convert to regular accounts on 18th birthday

### Minimum Balance & Charges
| Account Type | Minimum Balance | Non-maintenance Charge |
|---|---|---|
| Metro Savings | $1,000 | $200/month |
| Semi-urban Savings | $500 | $100/month |
| Rural Savings | $250 | $50/month |
| Business Current | $5,000 | $500/month |

### Dormant Account Policy
- Account becomes dormant after 12 months of inactivity
- Revive by visiting a branch with valid KYC
- Unclaimed deposits transferred to RBI's Depositor Education and Awareness Fund after 10 years

## Card Terms

### Card Liability
- Lost/stolen card: Liability capped if reported within 24 hours
- Unauthorized online transactions: Zero liability if 2FA was active
- Customer is liable for transactions made before reporting loss

### Card Misuse
- In case of PIN sharing, customer bears full liability
- SecureBank is not responsible for merchant processing errors

## Digital Banking Terms

### Login Security
- Customers must not share login credentials
- SecureBank is not liable for unauthorized access due to customer negligence
- Sessions expire after 10 minutes of inactivity

### Transaction Finality
- Fund transfers once initiated cannot be reversed
- IMPS/RTGS transfers are final and irrevocable
- UPI disputes must be raised within 60 days

## Loan Terms

### Prepayment
- Floating rate loans: No prepayment charges
- Fixed rate loans: 2% of prepaid amount
- Minimum prepayment: 1 EMI amount

### Default & Recovery
- EMI bounced: $500 bounce charge + penal interest 2% p.a.
- 90 days of non-payment: Account classified as NPA
- Legal recovery proceedings after 90 days NPA

## Governing Law
All disputes are subject to Mumbai jurisdiction and governed by Indian law.

## Amendment of Terms

- Notice on securebank.com

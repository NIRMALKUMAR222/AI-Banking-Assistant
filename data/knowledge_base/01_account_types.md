# SecureBank Account Types & Features

## Overview
SecureBank offers four primary account types designed to meet diverse customer needs.

## 1. Savings Account
A Savings Account is ideal for individuals looking to grow their money while maintaining liquidity.

**Key Features:**
- Minimum balance: $500
- Interest rate: 3.5% per annum (compounded quarterly)
- Free ATM withdrawals: 5 per month (thereafter $2 per withdrawal)
- Online banking and mobile app access included
- Auto-sweep to Fixed Deposit when balance exceeds $50,000

**Eligibility:**
- Indian resident individuals aged 18 and above
- Valid KYC documents required

## 2. Checking (Current) Account
A Checking Account suits everyday transactional needs for individuals and small businesses.

**Key Features:**
- No minimum balance for premium tier customers
- Standard minimum balance: $1,000
- Unlimited cheque transactions
- Overdraft facility up to $10,000 (subject to approval)
- Real-time payment notifications via SMS and email

## 3. Fixed Deposit (FD) Account
Fixed Deposits offer higher interest rates in exchange for locking funds for a specified term.

**Interest Rates:**
| Tenure         | Interest Rate |
|----------------|---------------|
| 7 – 30 days    | 4.00%         |
| 31 – 90 days   | 5.50%         |
| 91 – 180 days  | 6.25%         |
| 181 – 365 days | 7.00%         |
| 1 – 2 years    | 7.50%         |
| 2 – 5 years    | 7.75%         |
| 5+ years       | 8.00%         |

**Key Features:**
- Loan against FD up to 90% of principal
- Premature withdrawal allowed with 1% penalty
- Auto-renewal option available

## 4. Current Account (Business)
For businesses requiring high-volume transactions.

**Key Features:**
- Minimum balance: $5,000
- Unlimited transactions per month
- Cash management services
- Multi-currency support
- Dedicated relationship manager for premium accounts

## Account Maintenance Fees
- SMS alerts: Free
- Monthly statement by email: Free
- Physical statement: $2 per statement
- Cheque book (25 leaves): Free first two per year, then $5

## How to Open an Account
1. Visit any SecureBank branch or apply online at securebank.com
2. Submit KYC documents (Aadhaar, PAN, passport-size photo)
3. Make initial deposit
4. Receive account kit within 3–5 working days

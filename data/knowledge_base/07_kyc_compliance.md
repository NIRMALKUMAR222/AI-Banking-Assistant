# SecureBank KYC & Compliance

## Know Your Customer (KYC) Policy

SecureBank follows RBI-mandated KYC norms to prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and fraud.

## Documents Required for KYC

### Proof of Identity (Any one)
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Voter ID
- Driving License
- NREGA Job Card (for rural customers)
- PAN Card (mandatory for accounts with annual credit > $200,000)

### Proof of Address (Any one)
- Aadhaar Card
- Passport
- Utility bill (not older than 3 months)
- Bank statement (from another bank, not older than 3 months)
- Rent agreement registered with government

### Additional Documents (Situational)
- For minors: Birth certificate + guardian KYC
- For NRIs: Passport + visa/OCI card + overseas address proof
- For businesses: Certificate of Incorporation, MOA/AOA, beneficial ownership declaration

## KYC Updation
- Full KYC renewal: Every 10 years for low-risk customers
- Every 8 years for medium-risk customers
- Every 2 years for high-risk customers (PEPs, high-value accounts)

## Video KYC (V-KYC)
SecureBank accepts Video KYC for remote onboarding:
1. Customer connects via a live video call with a SecureBank officer
2. Original documents shown on camera
3. Aadhaar OTP verified in real-time
4. Facial match with Aadhaar photo

## Anti-Money Laundering (AML)
- All cash transactions above $50,000 are reported to FIU-India
- Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) filed for unusual patterns
- Customer Due Diligence (CDD) and Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) for high-risk accounts

## FATCA / CRS Compliance
SecureBank collects tax residency information to comply with:
- FATCA (Foreign Account Tax Compliance Act) — US customers
- CRS (Common Reporting Standard) — global auto-exchange of financial information

## Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs)
PEPs are subject to Enhanced Due Diligence:
- Source of wealth declaration required
- Higher monitoring frequency
- Senior management approval for account opening

## Customer Consent & Data Privacy
- SecureBank processes data per the Information Technology Act, 2000 and RBI guidelines
- Customers may request data access or deletion via privacy@securebank.com

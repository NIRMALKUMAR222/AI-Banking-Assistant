# SecureBank Credit Cards

## Card Portfolio

### 1. SecureBank Platinum Rewards Card
Best for: Everyday spenders who want maximum cashback.

**Key Benefits:**
- 5% cashback on grocery and dining
- 2% cashback on all other purchases
- Annual fee: $999 (waived on annual spend > $100,000)
- Credit limit: $50,000 – $500,000
- Complimentary airport lounge access: 4 visits/year
- Zero fraud liability on unauthorized transactions

### 2. SecureBank Travel Elite Card
Best for: Frequent flyers and travel enthusiasts.

**Key Benefits:**
- 10x reward points on flight and hotel bookings
- 3x points on dining and entertainment
- Annual fee: $4,999 (waived on annual spend > $500,000)
- Complimentary travel insurance up to $10,000,000
- Unlimited airport lounge access (Visa Signature)
- Global concierge service

### 3. SecureBank Shoppers Card
Best for: Online shoppers.

**Key Benefits:**
- 10% instant discount on partner e-commerce platforms
- EMI conversion on purchases > $5,000 at 0% interest (3/6 months)
- Annual fee: $499 (waived on annual spend > $50,000)
- No-cost EMI on 500+ partner brands

### 4. SecureBank Classic Debit Card
Best for: Day-to-day transactions and ATM access.

**Key Benefits:**
- Free on all savings and checking accounts
- Daily ATM withdrawal limit: $20,000
- Daily purchase limit: $100,000
- Chip + PIN + contactless (tap-to-pay)
- Visa/Mastercard/Rupay network

## Reward Points System
- 1 reward point = $0.25 redemption value
- Redemption options: Flight miles, hotel stays, shopping vouchers, cashback
- Points expiry: 36 months from date of earning

## Card Security Features
- Virtual card for online transactions
- Instant card freeze/unfreeze via mobile app
- OTP-based international transactions
- 3D Secure authentication for online purchases
- Real-time transaction alerts

## Credit Limit Enhancement
Customers can request a credit limit increase after 6 months of satisfactory usage by:
1. Submitting a request via the SecureBank app
2. Providing updated income proof
3. Approval within 3 working days

## Minimum Payment
- Minimum due: 5% of outstanding balance or $500 (whichever is higher)
- Interest on carried balance: 3.4% per month (40.8% p.a.)
- Late payment fee: $500 – $1,500 based on outstanding amount
